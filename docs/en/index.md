<p class="section-head__kicker">Direction</p>
<h2 class="section-head__title">Mission and vision</h2>
<p><strong>Mission.</strong> Towards high-quality software systems and a better development experience with Compiler 2.0 and Software 2.0.</p>
<p><strong>Vision.</strong> To be a national leader in software engineering research and education, recognised for AUT's contributions to innovative solutions that transform the software industry.</p>
</div>
<div markdown>

<li>Collaboration — partnerships with industry, academia, and government.</li>
